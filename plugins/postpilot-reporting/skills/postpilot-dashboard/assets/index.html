How to read this page

Everything here recalculates from your Campaign Data sheet each time the page loads.

iROAS and iRevenue
iROAS is one number per campaign over its whole life, and it only exists where a control group ran. iRevenue is the revenue that number implies; iRPR is the same per postcard.
A blank iROAS
Either the campaign had no control group, so its lift is unmeasured (adding one is how it gets an iROAS), or it had one and the mailed customers did no better than the control group. It never means zero.
Recent months
Orders count for 48 days after mailing and postcards take about a week to land. Recent sends are still collecting orders and are not a drop in performance.
More orders than postcards
Every order a mailed customer places within 48 days counts, repeat orders included. That is also why some one-time customer sends show very high ROAS.
